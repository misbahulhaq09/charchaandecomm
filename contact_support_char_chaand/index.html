FREQUENTLY ADDRESSED INQUIRIES

Atelier Governance & Common Queries

Comprehensive answers regarding couture timelines, precious jewels, and our atelier services.

Every bridal commission initiates with a dedicated consultation—either at our Mehrauli Flagship or via private digital video conference. Following silhouette curation, a Master Karigar and draper are assigned to your docket. Hand-embroidery, zardozi needlework, and fabric dye require 6 to 8 weeks, culminating in in-suite trial fittings prior to wedding dispatch.
All Char Chaand High Jewellery creations are certified with BIS Hallmarking and accompanied by international gemological certificates (IGI / GIA). Handover is completed through armored vault couriers requiring OTP authentication, biometric verification, and government identification. Every consignment carries 100% transit insurance underwritten by Lloyd's.
Alterations to silhouette specifications or delivery dates may be made within 48 hours of order formulation without impact on the delivery window. For adjustments post-loom production, contact your assigned client liaison or call our direct hotline at +91 11 4982 0000 citing your unique dossier reference.
We dispatch internationally to the United States, United Kingdom, UAE, Singapore, Canada, and Europe via DHL Couture Express. All local duties, tariffs, and luxury import assessments can be prepaid during order docket formulation to ensure seamless white-glove arrival at your private residence.
Pret-a-porter pieces in unworn condition with vault seals unsevered are eligible for exchange or atelier credit within 7 days of verified consignment receipt. Custom couture bridal lehengas and bespoke sherwanis are non-refundable, but carry unlimited complimentary fitment revisions until your bridal silhouette is perfected.
