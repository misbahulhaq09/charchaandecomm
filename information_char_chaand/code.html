Terms of Service & Atelier Conditions

Standard operational covenants governing intellectual property, zardozi motifs, formulation windows, and Mehrauli jurisdictional sovereignty.

1. Intellectual Property & Archival Motifs

All jaal graphs, zardozi patterns, marodi embroidery formulations, and handloom silhouettes are the proprietary intellectual property of Char Chaand Haute Couture Pvt. Ltd. Unauthorized commercial reproduction, digital vectorization, or manufacturing replication is strictly prohibited under the Indian Copyright Act, 1957.

2. Artisanal Weft Variations

Because our textiles are woven by hand on traditional Banarasi and Chanderi pit looms, subtle textural variations, slubs in raw tussar silk, and natural vegetal dye inflections are intrinsic signatures of artisanal authenticity rather than manufacturing discrepancies.

3. 24-Hour Formulation Window

Due to the immediate purchase of raw mulberry silk and authentic silver zari wire upon order ratification, cancellations for bespoke and made-to-measure orders can only be accepted within exactly 24 hours of checkout. Following this interval, raw material cutting commences in our karkhana.

4. Legal Jurisdiction

All agreements, transactions, and covenants entered through this digital domain shall be governed exclusively by the laws of the Republic of India. Any claims or judicial disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in New Delhi.

HEIRLOOM CONSERVATION

Preserving Your Trousseau Across Generations

Real zari woven with pure silver wire and dipped in 24k gold requires specialized archival stewardship. Follow our master conservators’ guidance to ensure your bridal ensemble endures as an heirloom.

  • check_circle Pure Muslin Swaddling: Never store pure zari lehengas in plastic garment bags; use unbleached cambric muslin to allow silk breathability.
  • check_circle Neem Leaves & Cedar: Employ organic sun-dried neem leaves in muslin pouches rather than mothballs, which tarnish precious silver threads.
  • check_circle Refolding Cadence: Unfold and refold hand-embroidered bridal garments every 6 months along alternative crease lines to prevent metal fiber fracture.
