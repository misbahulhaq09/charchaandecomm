The Char Champagne Lehenga translates ancient royal court attire into an ethereal contemporary silhouette. Crafted over 450 artisanal karigar hours, the skirt features architectural Kalis panelled with real metallic wire needlecraft, dabka, sitara, and micro-pearl inlays.

Woven on centuries-old pit-looms in Varanasi, the sheer tissue base creates a fluid, light-refracting luminescence that moves with stately grace under ambient banquet lighting.

  • Base Fabric: 100% Pure Mulberry Silk Tissue with Satin Crepe lining.
  • Embroidery: Handcrafted Zardozi, Cutdana, French Knots & 24k Gold Electroplated Zari.
  • Blouse: Padded corseted neckline with concealed heirloom closure and hand-beaded latkans.
  • Dupatta: 2.75m scalloped border veil in fine champagne silk organza.
  • Care Instructions: Strictly specialist dry clean only. Preserve in the Char Chaand complimentary muslin storage box away from direct moisture.

Each couture ensemble is individualized to your exact silhouette. Upon order confirmation, our senior draper contacts you to collect 18 bespoke bridal measurements or coordinate a private fitting in our New Delhi, Mumbai, or London salons.

Need urgent delivery? Standard size orders ship within 14 working days.

Complimentary insured door-to-door courier on all bridal commissions globally. Each order is packed in cedar-lined archival travel trunks.

Because every couture garment is customized, we do not accept standard returns; however, complimentary adjustments and master tailor re-fittings are fully guaranteed.
