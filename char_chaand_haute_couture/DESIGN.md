## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal grandeur of Indian haute couture translated through the restraint of modern European editorial design. It addresses high-net-worth patrons, bridal connoisseurs, and international luxury fashion buyers seeking bespoke bridal lehengas, sherwanis, and artisanal pret-a-porter.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Editorial Minimalism meets Regal Opulence:** A stark departure from busy, cluttered marketplaces. Generous negative space acts as a gilded frame around high-resolution textile photography, zari details, and intricate zardozi embroidery.
- **Architectural Poise:** Sharp, architectural lines combined with hairline dividers, classic proportioning, and deliberate symmetry inspired by Mughal archways and royal heritage architecture.
- **Tone & Mood:** Reverent, sophisticated, heirloom-quality, and deeply refined. Every interaction communicates tactility, artisan craft, and quiet prestige.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from royal Indian court attire, vintage silks, and unbleached parchment:

### Color Roles & Distribution
- **Primary (`#4A0E17` / Royal Burgundy):** The core brand anchor. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key interactive links, and strong brand presence. Evokes velvet tapestries and heritage rubies.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880` / Muted Champagne Gold):** Used for delicate hairline borders, refined category tags, decorative iconography, rating accents, and subtle secondary dividers. Represents antique zari and beaten gold work.
- **Tertiary (`#8B2635` / Rose Zari):** A vibrant crimson-rose accent reserved for rare promotional highlights, bridal badges, and subtle hover interactions.
- **Neutral Dark (`#221314` / Espresso Wine):** Deep, warm near-black replacement for all high-contrast typography, editorial body copy, and solid dark narrative panels.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FBF9F5` / Raw Ivory Silk): Primary background throughout the experience.
  - Secondary Canvas (`#F5F0EB` / Chanderi Mist): Subdued surface for product cards, collection carousels, and modular panels.
  - Tertiary Canvas (`#EFE8DF` / Warm Parchment): Used for footer blocks, newsletter banners, and subtle alternating editorial story strips.
  - Border Hairline (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.28)` / Muted Gold Hairline): 1px non-distracting visual boundaries.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts the dramatic, romantic elegance of high-fashion serif titles with a clear, airy modern sans-serif.

### Type Pairing
- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (`Playfair Display`):** High-contrast transitional serif with classical proportions. Renders titles, collection names, artisan quotes, and dramatic lead-ins with deliberate editorial grace.
- **Body & Navigation (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, crisp, modern geometric sans. Delivers effortless legibility for garment specifications, pricing, luxury product filters, and checkout fields.
- **Tracking & Text Treatment:** All button text, breadcrumbs, top banner alerts, and category badges must be rendered in uppercase (`text-transform: uppercase`) with tracked spacing (`letterSpacing: 0.12em` to `0.18em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column fixed/fluid hybrid grid with generous outer margins to mimic luxury coffee table monographs and editorial lookbooks.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapters
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column structure with `4rem` outer margin and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas bounded at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column structure with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column structure with `1.25rem` outer margin and `0.75rem` gutters.

### Editorial Composition
- Category carousels adopt asymmetric ratios with round circular story previews contrasting against tall vertical aspect-ratio garment cards (`3:4` or `4:5`).
- Editorial campaign banners span 2-column split features combining full-bleed studio imagery with calm ivory text modules.
- Generous vertical whitespace separates editorial moments, preserving a serene browsing cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy, muddy dropshadows in favor of flat editorial refinement, tonal layering, and hair-thin gold borders.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Raw Ivory (`#FBF9F5`) flat background plane.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Subtle tonal shifts to Soft Mist (`#F5F0EB`) or Warm Parchment (`#EFE8DF`) paired with a 1px border of `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.35)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Float States):** Garment cards elevate strictly through an ultra-soft, diffused amber-tinted shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Drawers):** Cart drawer, bridal consultation modals, and mega-menus utilize pure ivory surfaces framed with delicate antique gold rules and an ambient backdrop scrim of `rgba(34, 19, 20, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Sharp (`0`)** shape language for architectural luxury, complemented by deliberate perfect circles strictly for visual storytelling previews and icon buttons.

### Architectural Rules
- **Buttons, Cards, Inputs, and Modals:** `0px` radius (crisp corners). Reflects tailoring patterns, high-fashion invitation envelopes, and fine stationery.
- **Story / Highlight Avatars:** Perfect circular silhouettes (`border-radius: 9999px`) exclusively reserved for artisan profiles, category medallions, and testimonial portraits as shown in luxury runway features.
- **Badges & Tags:** Sharp rectilinear boxes with minimal hairline borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#4A0E17` (Deep Burgundy), text `#FBF9F5` (Ivory), 0px corner radius, font `label-md` in uppercase with `0.15em` letter spacing. Padding: `14px 28px`. Subtle hover shift to `#36070E`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background transparent, 1px solid `#4A0E17`, text `#4A0E17`. On hover: fills to `#4A0E17` with text `#FBF9F5`.
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Text `#221314` with an accompanying subtle arrow (`→`), underlined with a hairline gold border spaced 4px below the text.

### Garment & Product Cards
- Aspect ratio: `3:4` vertical portrait format.
- Subtle `#F5F0EB` matte background behind unclipped couture photography.
- Floating wishlist heart icon rendered in fine gold stroke (`#C5A880`) positioned top-right with `12px` inset.
- Typography stack: Garment title in `body-md` (Plus Jakarta Sans, semi-bold), followed by collection note and price in `title-md` (`#4A0E17`).

### Input Fields & Newsletter Subscription
- Sharp rectilinear fields with 1px border in `#C5A880` or `rgba(34, 19, 20, 0.2)`.
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#FBF9F5`.
- Floating labels using `label-sm` in tracked uppercase. Focus state highlights the border with `#4A0E17`.

### Story / Category Medallions
- Circular image wrappers (`rounded-full`, 80px to 100px diameter) nested in `#F5F0EB` background with a hairline ring (`1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.4)`).
- Centered caption underneath in `body-sm` (Espresso Wine).

### Testimonial & Editorial Quotation Cards
- Centered editorial layout featuring 5 filled micro-stars in Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`).
- Customer quote rendered in `body-md` with `Playfair Display` quotation marks.
- Reviewer attribution in `label-sm` with a preceding em-dash.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Sharp, square checkboxes (`0px` radius) with 1px border `#C5A880`. Checked state fills with `#4A0E17` and a white checkmark.
- Circular radio buttons with a nested solid circle indicator in `#4A0E17`.
